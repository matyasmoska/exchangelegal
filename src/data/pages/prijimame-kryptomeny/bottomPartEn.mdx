# **We accept cryptocurrencies (virtual assets)**

You can pay for all our services (in addition to fiat currency) also in common virtual assets – cryptocurrencies.

## **Which virtual assets do we accept?**

As standard, we accept **Bitcoin, Litecoin, Ether, USDC, Monero and Dogecoin**.

If you would like to pay in another virtual asset, <a href="/kontakty">let us know</a> and we will try to accommodate you.

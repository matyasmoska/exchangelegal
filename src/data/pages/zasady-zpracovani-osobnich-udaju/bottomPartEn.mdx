## 3. Purposes and legal bases

### Responding to an enquiry and pre-contractual steps

We process data to answer an enquiry, assess whether we can accept the matter, perform conflict checks and prepare a proposal. The legal basis is taking steps at the data subject's request prior to entering into a contract and, where necessary, our legitimate interest in managing and documenting enquiries.

### Legal services

Once a matter has been accepted, we process data to perform the engagement, comply with an advocate's legal duties, protect the client's rights and establish, exercise or defend legal claims. Additional information may be provided in a client-specific privacy notice.

### Legal obligations

We process data where required by legislation, including rules on the legal profession, accounting, tax, AML and duties towards public authorities.

### Website security and legal claims

We process necessary technical logs based on our legitimate interests in website security, prevention of misuse, incident response and protection of legal claims.

### Analytics and marketing

Non-essential analytics and marketing tools are used only on the basis of consent. Consent may be changed or withdrawn at any time through the **Cookie settings** link in the footer. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing before withdrawal.

### Legal updates and commercial communications

Where you subscribe separately, we process your email address and the consent record to send legal updates. Existing clients may receive communications concerning similar services where permitted by law, always with a simple opt-out.

**Implementation instruction:** If the website does not in fact offer a newsletter, remove this subsection and all newsletter checkboxes.

## 4. Is provision of data mandatory?

Providing data through the contact form is voluntary. Without basic contact details and a description of the matter, however, we may be unable to respond. Mandatory fields must be limited to what is genuinely necessary.

Do not send special categories of personal data, criminal-offence data or sensitive documents through the initial form unless they are necessary for a preliminary assessment. A more secure channel may be agreed for further communication.

## 5. Retention

We retain data only for as long as necessary for the relevant purpose:

- an ordinary enquiry that does not lead to an engagement: generally no more than 12 months after the last communication;
- a record required to document a conflict check or refusal of a matter: for the period necessary to protect rights and comply with professional duties;
- client files: during the engagement and thereafter for the period required by law, professional rules or the protection of legal claims;
- accounting and tax records: for the applicable statutory periods;
- AML records, where applicable: for the period required by the AML legislation;
- security logs: generally no more than 90 days, unless required for an incident or legal claim;
- analytics user and event data: no more than 14 months in the analytics tool settings;
- consent records: for the duration of the consent and a proportionate period afterwards to demonstrate that it was obtained, generally no more than three years;
- marketing contacts: until consent is withdrawn or communications are rejected.

Where a dispute, inspection or other proceeding arises, relevant data may be kept for the duration of that matter and the associated limitation or retention periods.
